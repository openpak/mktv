About this site

OpenKart is OpenPak's own site for Mario Kart 8 (Wii U), Mario Kart 8 Deluxe (Switch), Mario Kart 7 (3DS) and Mario Kart Wii. It reads what each game uploaded to its OpenPak server and shows it: time-trial rankings for each course, ghosts you can download, and, where the game has them, community tournaments and Mario Kart TV highlights (Mario Kart 8 only).

A game appears in the top bar once its server is connected to this site.

Your results

Sign in with your OpenPak account to see your own times. The site finds your player in each game from the consoles linked to your account on openpak.org; a game whose console is not linked says so, with a link to link it.

What it cannot show

  • Replays. A highlight (the game's Mario Kart TV recording) is a recording the game replays with its own engine. Nothing outside the game can play it, so the site shows the details and thumbnail the game uploads with it, and a video if the player published one.
  • Ghosts. Ghost files can be downloaded but only play in their own game.
  • Course names. Mario Kart Wii courses are named. For the other games the number the game uses for a course is not yet matched to a course name, so those pages say "Course 7" and so on until it is confirmed on a console.
  • Names. Players appear by player ID unless the game stores a name (Mario Kart Wii does).
